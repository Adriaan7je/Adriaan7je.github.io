Privacy policy

Roger is a private assistant used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google accounts.

  • Roger asks Google only for read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change mail.
  • Mail is processed on the owner's own server to decide what matters to the owner. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Roger's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
